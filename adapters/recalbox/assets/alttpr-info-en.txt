**A Link to the Past Randomizer**

Every seed reshuffles Nintendo's 1991 classic into a new puzzle while preserving
the original SNES adventure.

__**Project**__
- Original game: Nintendo
- Randomizer engine: codemann8's ALttPDoorRandomizer
- Based on: Aerinon's Door Randomizer and the ALttPR community
- Recalbox integration: jscaravilli

__**This build**__
- Native Python 3.11 seed generation
- Item, dungeon, door, entrance, and overworld shuffle support
- Controller-driven configuration and seed library
- Spoiler viewer, custom sprites, MSU-1 music, and phone autotracker

Choose **Generate Custom Seed** to configure a run, create it locally, and launch
straight into the game.
