**What is ALTTPR?**

ALTTPR reshuffles items and progression in Nintendo's 1991 SNES adventure,
*The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past*. Each generated seed turns the familiar
world into a new puzzle, with optional dungeon, door, entrance, and overworld
shuffles. Choose **Generate Custom Seed** to configure and play.
