ALTTPR Community Sprite Assets
================================

Source catalog:
https://alttpr.com/sprites

Alternate catalog and combined preview sheet reference:
http://alttp.mymm1.com/sprites/?metadata=1

The bundled ZSPR files and preview images are the ALTTPR community sprite
catalog snapshot used by this release. Sprite names and authors are preserved
in bin/data/sprites.json.src and in the generated menu manifest. The user has
confirmed these assets are available under MIT usage terms.
